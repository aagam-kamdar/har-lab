6. SEPARATION OF VERIFIED FACTS VS. LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED FACTS:
- YOLOv8-Pose detects +496 (+7.3%) more person bounding boxes over 1800 frames.
- Spatial agreement is high on shared detections (Mean IoU: 0.770, 89.7% >= 0.50 IoU).
- During difficult desk occlusion windows, Pose reduces missed frames (Aagam: 0 vs 3; Harshil: 49 vs 267).
- Current YOLO is faster on CPU (28.9 FPS vs 29.2 FPS).

LIMITATIONS / UNVERIFIED ASSUMPTIONS:
- Model-capacity mismatch: YOLOv8-Pose contains 3.29M parameters vs 2.57M for Current YOLO (+28.1% extra parameters in keypoint head).
- Keypoints were discarded, meaning the computational cost of keypoint branch is incurred without identity benefit in this test.
- Increased box count includes occasional partial-body detections that may challenge downstream tracker associations.

7. EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION: Pose improves difficult-case recall (recovering partially occluded desk poses), but costs ~20-30% more CPU time.
The benchmark results support further consideration/testing in integration scenarios, but do NOT justify replacing the production detector automatically without end-to-end tracking evaluation.
