-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- YOLO26-Pose increases person detections by +34 (+0.5%).

LIMITATIONS / UNVERIFIED ASSUMPTIONS:
- Parameter disparity: YOLO26-Pose has +43.0% more parameters (3.68M vs 2.57M) strictly due to the 17-keypoint Pose26 head.
- Keypoints were discarded, incurring Pose26 head computation without utilizing anatomical joints downstream.
- Downstream tracker impact is unverified: while more person boxes are detected, their impact on ByteTrack tracklet continuity requires separate pipeline evaluation.

7. EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION: When controlling strictly for model generation (YOLO26 vs YOLO26-Pose), the Pose variant demonstrates genuine difficult-case recall improvement during seated desk/monitor occlusion (recovering Harshil in 200+ frames), while incurring a modest CPU latency overhead (~34ms vs ~43ms). Per instructions, production code remains unmodified.
